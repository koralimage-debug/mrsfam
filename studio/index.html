HOW TO DO A PHONE INTERVIEW

A quiet room.
A clear plan.

This Studio records the microphone on this device. It does not join a phone call by itself.

  1. Wear wired earbuds or headphones

    Wired earbuds keep your voice out of the speakers and help you hear yourself if you turn monitoring on. A quiet room with soft furnishings and notifications off will sound cleaner.

  2. Place the guest on a second device

    Ask your guest to join by a regular phone call on a second phone, or put that call on speaker a short distance away. This phone then hears both of you through its microphone. Avoid putting the same phone on speaker while this page is open — that can cause echo or a feedback squeal.

  3. Open the microphone and do a short test

    Allow microphone access when the browser asks. Speak in your normal on-air voice and watch the level meter move. Try Radio Voice first, then Warm or Clear Interview if you want a different sound.

  4. Record, then download

    Confirm you have permission to record everyone, start the music bed if you want it, tap Record, and use the pads for a station ID, intro, applause or outro. The ON AIR light stays on until you tap Stop. Download the file straight away.

  5. Going live on Mrs Fam Radio later

    Mrs Fam Radio is already on air 24/7 with its regular programming. Broadcasting a live show from a phone or computer needs a RadioKing Start plan or higher, plus an encoder app. That upgrade has not been purchased yet, so use this Studio to record interviews you can upload afterwards. We are not listing plan prices here.
