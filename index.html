ABOUT US · MEET THE FOUNDER

Founded by Olayinka Omotayo Fambegbe (Mrs Fam), a qualified social worker, broadcaster, wife and mother, Real Talk with Mrs Fam is an inclusive, community-focused platform dedicated to honest, meaningful and thought-provoking conversations about real-life experiences and issues affecting individuals, families and society.

The platform creates space for people to share experiences, learn, reflect and see things from different perspectives.

Our aim is to encourage open conversations about subjects that are sometimes uncomfortable, misunderstood or rarely discussed, without judgement or sugarcoating. We welcome different opinions, backgrounds, cultures and generations.

Let’s solve the small things.

Microphone or camera won’t start

Allow microphone and camera access in your browser’s site settings. Close other apps using the device, reconnect it, then test again.

Echo or feedback

Use headphones, mute duplicate call tabs and keep only one active microphone per room.

Audio is too quiet or distorted

Check the selected input in your system settings. Move closer if quiet; lower input gain if distorted. Record a short test and listen back.

Remote guest connection is unstable

Try wired internet, close background uploads and use a provider that records each guest locally. Browser recording here does not capture a remote call.

What equipment belongs in the setup?

Start with a computer, a compatible USB microphone, wired headphones and a quiet room. A stable camera mount and soft front light help for video. Test what you already own before upgrading.

Guided troubleshooting only; this site cannot control or repair a guest’s device remotely.
